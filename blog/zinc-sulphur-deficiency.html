Zinc + Sulphur deficiency: photo-style symptom guide (जिंक + सल्फर की कमी)

Zinc (जिंक) — khaira disease in wheat

Signs: dusty yellow-white patches on young leaves 2–3 weeks after transplanting/sowing; stunted tillers; delayed maturity. Common in sandy, high-pH, continuously flooded patches.

Correction (indicative): basal zinc sulphate 10–12 kg/acre at sowing or foliar 0.5% zinc sulphate + lime spray at tillering (evening). Confirm dose at counter.

Sulphur (सल्फर) — pale mustard, low oil

Signs: whole-plant pale green/yellow (unlike nitrogen which yellows old leaves first); thin stems; poor pod set in mustard.

Correction (indicative): basal sulphur 15–20 kg/acre via SSP/sulphur-bentonite as advised; mustard responds strongly.
