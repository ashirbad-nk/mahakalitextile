Fertilizer price & subsidy basics (2026)

Urea is tightly price-controlled (cheapest N); DAP/NPK/MOP follow Nutrient-Based Subsidy rates revised seasonally + global prices. That's why DAP moves but urea barely does.

How to buy smart

1) Ask today's rate (never last month's). 2) Compare per-kg nutrient, not per-bag. 3) Insist on bill + batch. 4) Beware "subsidy scam" calls — subsidy goes to companies, never to your bank via link.
