Organic + chemical combo: cut cost, heal soil (जैविक + रासायनिक)

Pure chemical every season hardens sandy-loam and wastes money. A 3-step combo works for most Abu Road fields:

1) Base: 2–4 quintal vermicompost/FYM per acre before sowing (moisture + microbes).
2) Trim: cut basal DAP ~10–15% where soil P is medium (confirm via Soil Card).
3) Boost: seed treatment + PSB/Azotobacter culture + need-based urea splits.

Farmers typically see input savings from season 2 onward — plus better water holding in summer.
