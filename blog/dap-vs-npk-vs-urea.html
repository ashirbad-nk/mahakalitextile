DAP vs NPK vs Urea — kab kya dalein? (डीएपी vs एनपीके vs यूरिया)

BagFeedsWhen
DAP 18-46-0Roots + early growth (P-heavy)Basal at sowing only
NPK (10:26:26 etc.)Balanced N+P+KBasal where K/S needed; mustard, pulses
Urea 46-0-0Leaf + tiller growth (N only)Top-dress with irrigation, 2 splits

Bag numbers in 30 seconds

18-46-0 = 18% N, 46% phosphate, 0% potash per 100 kg. So 50 kg DAP ≈ 9 kg N + 23 kg phosphate. Full decode: how to read a bag →

Simple rule

Sowing time → DAP/NPK (neeche ki khad). Growing time → Urea (upar ki khad) with water. Mustard/pulses lean NPK; wheat leans DAP + urea splits.

Can I replace DAP fully with NPK?

Sometimes — if soil K is low, 12:32:16 can substitute DAP basal. Ask with your Soil Card on WhatsApp.
