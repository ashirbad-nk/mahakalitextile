How to read a fertilizer bag (N-P-K) in 5 minutes

N-P-K = Nitrogen (पत्ती/वृद्धि), Phosphate (जड़/फूल), Potash (दाना/रोग-रोध). 18-46-0 means per 100 kg: 18 kg N + 46 kg phosphate + 0 potash.

Quick math

50 kg DAP (18-46-0) ≈ 9 kg N + 23 kg P. 50 kg urea (46-0-0) ≈ 23 kg N. That's why 1 bag DAP basal + 2 urea splits balances wheat.

At the counter, check 4 things

1) Sealed stitching 2) MRP + batch + mfg date 3) Grade printed (not handwritten) 4) GST bill with same batch. We show all 4 before you pay.
