## 1. Palanpur Market Context (what we found)

- **Core retail belt:** Station Road, Amir Road, Delhi Gate, Kirti Stambh, Panchratna Market, Municipal Market. Any Palanpur shopper knows these landmarks — site must name them for trust + Local SEO.
  - Example: Bhagwati Saree Showroom: Kirti Stambh, Railway Station Road, Palanpur — open 10 AM-8 PM, claims largest showroom in North Gujarat since 1998 (Deesa → Palanpur).
- **Competitor density:** 10+ saree/clothing stores in 2 km radius (Suresh Garments, Shree Mateshwari Sarees, Jay Laxmi Saree Selection, Rohit Textiles, Rajkumar Mall, Durga Emporium, Sathiya NX, Zara Fashion Palanpur, Toran/Amrapali/Nagarvel Saree Centers). Most compete on variety + price. None found with strong content/SEO storytelling — gap for luxury positioning.
- **Review leader to beat:** Phoenix Fashion Palanpur — 756 ratings at 5.0, S9 Imperial, Palanpur Rd, active Instagram @phoenix.palanpur. Rajkumar Mall — 233 ratings 4.6. Most others <10 ratings. Learning: active photos + Instagram + reviews = local pack win in Palanpur.
- **Luxury opportunity:** Palanpur sits near Patan (world-famous Patan Patola, ~60 km) and Kutch Bandhani belt. No Palanpur competitor owns the story "authentic Patola / Bandhani guidance + bridal styling in Palanpur." Mahakali can own: heritage weaves, pure-fabric education, private video selection, wedding family styling.
- **Buyer behaviour:** Mobile-only, WhatsApp-first, Hindi/Gujarati + English. 46% of Google searches are local; 76% of nearby smartphone searches lead to store visit within a day (BrightLocal/Google data via DotCreative, GlowingDigital). Photos on profile = +42% direction requests, +35% click-throughs. Reviews mentioning product ("bought Bandhani for daughter's wedding") boost ranking.
- **Languages:** English for portfolio + SEO titles, Hinglish captions for Instagram (2-3x engagement vs English-only per StitchMagic), Gujarati/Hindi lines for trust: "Palanpur ni heritage saree destination".

**Implication for site:** Don't try to be cheapest. Be most trusted for premium. Every section needs: craft proof, authenticity honesty, price bands, WhatsApp CTA.

## 4. What Content Is Best Suitable (recommendations per skill)

### 4.1 Digital Marketing (strategy block)
Show 30-60-90 day local plan, not vanity claims:
- Days 1-30: GBP setup + 20 photos + NAP consistency (JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha) + WhatsApp Business catalogue (20 SKUs) + review drive (target 30 reviews mentioning product).
- Days 31-60: 3 blogs live + 12 Reels + Pinterest boards + festival calendar (Navratri/Diwali/wedding season backwards planning).
- Days 61-90: Google Search + PMax test ₹500/day, retargeting, UGC loop (₹200 credit for customer photo — 40% participation benchmark from saree D2C case study).
Funnel visual: Discover (Search/Reels) → Chat (WhatsApp <30 min reply) → Visit (Maps + appointment) → Buy → Review (photo + product keyword).

### 4.2 SEO
Live demo section showing real title/meta/H1 outline + schema snippet + Core Web Vitals badge. 3 blogs (600-900 words each, FAQ + WhatsApp CTA):
1. Designer Saree Buying Guide in Palanpur (silk, zari, handwork checks)
2. Patola vs Bandhani: How to Identify Pure Handwork Before You Pay Premium
3. Bridal & Wedding Family Checklist — Budget-wise Luxury in Palanpur
Why these: directly answer premium buyer fears (fake, overpaying, wedding stress) and are uncontested vs generic category pages.

### 4.3 Social Media Marketing / Posting
Research consensus (2026): Reels = reach (3-5/week, 15-45s, 9:16, trending audio, on-screen price/fabric/order), Stories = daily trust (3-7 frames: arrivals, "only 2 left", polls, packing), Feed = catalogue (2-3/week, flat-lay + drape + 4 views: full, weave close-up, border/pallu, motion).
- 9-tile starter grid for site mock: heritage arrival, zari macro, drape reel cover, colour story, bridal styling, craft detail, review quote, festive lookbook, appointment invite.
- Caption formula: Hook + fabric/weave/length/blouse + colour honesty + price + CTA. Hinglish mix. 3-5 specific hashtags, not 30: e.g. #BandhaniSaree #PatanPatola #PalanpurShopping #BridalGujarat #BanarasiSilk + location tag Palanpur.
- Bio template: "Palanpur's luxury ethnic house | Patola • Bandhani • Banarasi • Bridal | WhatsApp 'HI' to +917978601417 👇" + chat link + Highlights: New, Bridal, Festive, Care, Delivery, Reviews.
- Proof rule from Banarasi case study: only claim "pure/handloom" if you can prove it; show Silk Mark/process or say power-loom honestly. Builds luxury trust.

### 4.4 Content Writing
Tone: warm, simple, expert, no jargon. Trilingual touches. Every product desc uses same 7-point template (saves inbox time): 1) type/fabric 2) weave/zari plainly 3) length/width/blouse 4) colour + screen variation note 5) care 6) price + includes 7) delivery/payment/exchange. Sample to write in build: 150-word Bandhani product story + 3 blog teasers. Add size/care highlight to cut returns 20-30%.

### 4.5 Mobile-First Design
Must-haves from research: 360px first, 48px tap targets, sticky bottom Call|WhatsApp|Directions bar (<768px), system fonts, lazy images, WebP <150KB, total JS <20KB, no carousel lib, <details> FAQ, Lighthouse Mobile >90. Evening 7-9 PM posting window matters — site must load fast on 4G then. QA via Playwright: no horizontal scroll at 360x800, links valid, alt present, JSON-LD parses.

### 4.6 Google Ads (sample numbers — label SAMPLE)
Benchmarks India 2025: fashion/apparel CPC ₹8-55, typical Search ₹20-50, CAC ₹200-1200 (GarmentVilla case: ₹443/order). Local Palanpur intent cheaper than Mumbai (+25-30% premium there). Recommend demo structure:
- Campaign A Search (intent capture, 40-50% budget): "designer saree shop palanpur", "bridal lehenga palanpur", "bandhani saree shop near me" + negatives: wholesale, second hand, used, jobs, free.
- Campaign B Performance Max (catalog, needs 50 conv/mo to exit learning — note as portfolio insight): top 10 SKUs with optimized feed titles Brand+Type+Attribute.
- Campaign C Local/Maps + Retargeting (highest ROI): site visitors + video viewers.
- Demo budget table: ₹500/day test (₹15k/mo) → ~300-600 clicks → 2% conv → 6-12 enquiries; scale only winners. Include JS ROI slider in build. Vernacular note: Hindi headlines +15-25% CTR in Tier 2/3. Festival pre-load 10-14 days before Diwali/Navratri/wedding peaks when CPC +40-60%.
